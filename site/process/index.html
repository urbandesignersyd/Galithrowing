Studio · Methodology

Process

Our method extends well beyond practical completion. Each phase carries its own horizon, and the last ones belong to people we will never meet.

  1. Day 0

    Listening

    We begin without drawings. We hear the client, the community and the site, and record what each needs and what each has lost.

  2. Weeks

    Reading the ground

    Geology, hydrology, ecology, history and Country. We read the site as a palimpsest of the layers that came before us.

  3. Months

    Drafting the long brief

    The client’s brief is rewritten alongside a five-hundred-year brief. Where the two conflict, the conflict becomes the design problem.

  4. Years

    Making

    Documentation, procurement and construction. Every assembly is drawn twice: once to build it and once to take it apart.

  5. Decades

    Stewardship

    A maintenance and adaptation manual written for future owners, tested on post-occupancy visits.

  6. Centuries

    Inheritance

    An archive of drawings, material sources and intent, prepared so that future custodians can repair, adapt and continue the work.
