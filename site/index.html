Gartrell Design · Galithrowing

Design for the five-hundredth year.

Galithrowing is a design practice that measures its work not in fiscal quarters but in centuries. We make places, objects and ideas intended to be inherited, repaired and loved by people not yet born.

500

Year horizon

Every brief is tested against a five-century masterplan, not a fifty-year lifecycle.

∞

Repairable by intent

We draw the disassembly as carefully as the assembly, so that the work can be mended rather than replaced.
